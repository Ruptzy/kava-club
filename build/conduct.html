Code of conduct

Every level belongs at these tables

Turning up to a club night, an event or the Discord means you agree to the clauses below. They apply to everyone, officers included.

01 · Casual and competitive both count

Nobody is shamed for not playing rated, and nobody is shamed for taking it seriously. Everyone has a rank. Nobody uses theirs as a weapon.

02 · No chess bullying

Hunting beginners to pad a rating is not a game. Stronger players are here to teach — raise people up, don't shoot them down.

03 · Win and lose the same way

Celebrate your wins, we want to hear about them. Just don't build them out of someone else's loss. Humility isn't thinking less of yourself, it's thinking of yourself less.

04 · Let people play their own game

No commentary on someone else's board unless both players invite it — no suggesting moves, no reading the position out loud. Unasked-for advice sets people back further than losing does.

05 · Keep it kind

Harassment of any kind, in person or in messages, ends your place here. Politics, religion and jokes about self-harm stay off the table. If you'd have to think twice before posting it, don't.

06 · US Chess rules apply

We are a US Chess affiliate, so the federation's Safe Play policy covers our club nights as well as our rated events, and their fair play rules govern every game we run.

07 · One promotion a day

On the Discord, every member gets one self-promotional post a day — streams, business, social media. Anything more, ask an admin first.

08 · Raise it with an officer

If something is wrong, say so. The club captain has the final say. Suggestions are welcome too — that is how half of these clauses came to exist.

How it is enforced

First strike

A word from an officer. Usually that is the end of it.

Second strike

You sit out the rest of the night, and an officer talks it through with you. On the Discord, a timeout.

Third strike

Suspended from club nights and the Discord for a set period.

Fourth strike

Removed from the club for good.

Harassment, cheating, or anything falling under US Chess Safe Play skips the scale entirely. The club captain has the final say.

The full rules, and the club chat, live on our Discord.
