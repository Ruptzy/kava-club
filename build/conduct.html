Code of conduct

Every level belongs at these tables

Attending a club night, an event or the Discord constitutes agreement to the clauses below. They apply to all members, officers included.

  1. Everyone is welcome

    This club is open to all. Harassment or discrimination on the basis of race, sex, sexuality, gender identity, religion, disability or age is prohibited, in person and online.

  2. All levels welcome

    Casual and competitive play are equally welcome. No member is judged for their rating or their style of play.

  3. No chess bullying

    Do not seek out weaker players for easy wins to feel good about yourself. Stronger players are here to teach.

  4. Sportsmanship

    Win and lose with respect. Boasting at another player's expense is not permitted.

  5. No unsolicited advice

    Do not comment on a game in progress unless both players agree. This includes suggesting moves and discussing the position.

  6. Disputes

    Serious disputes have never been part of this club, and the professionalism is something we are known for. Where a rated game is in question, the arbiter decides and the decision stands. Keep it friendly, respectful and classy.

  7. Keep debate off the board

    Politics, religion and self-harm are not topics for club spaces, in person or on the Discord.

  8. Age

    Club nights are 21 and over. Some events are open to 18 and over; when they are, the event listing says so.

  9. Alcohol-free

    The venue is alcohol-free and so is the club. Anyone who appears intoxicated will be asked to leave. Repeated incidents result in removal from the club.

  10. US Chess rules

    As a US Chess affiliate, the federation's Safe Play policy and fair play rules apply at club nights and at rated events.

  11. Promotion on the Discord

    One self-promotional post per member per day. Anything further requires an admin's approval.

  12. Reporting

    Raise concerns with an officer. The club director has the final say.

Enforcement

First

Verbal warning from an officer.

Second

Removed from play for the rest of the night. Timeout on the Discord.

Third

Temporary suspension from club nights and the Discord.

Fourth

Permanent removal from the club.

Harassment, cheating and Safe Play violations are not subject to this scale and may result in immediate suspension or removal.

The full rules, and the club chat, live on our Discord.
