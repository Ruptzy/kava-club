Quick questions

Do I need to know the rules?

No. We teach complete beginners too. Knowing how the pieces move just makes it easier.

Do I need a rating or a US Chess membership?

No. Those only matter for our rated tournaments.

Can I just watch?

Yes. Pull up a chair, just don't comment on a game in progress.

Will stronger players go easy on me?

They're here to teach, not to hunt easy wins. It's in our code of conduct.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. The director and several members are bilingual.
