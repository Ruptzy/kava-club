Quick questions

What is there to do in Bradenton at night besides bars?

Chess on the patio at Kava Social, Sundays and Tuesdays from 8PM. It's social, alcohol-free and open to every level.

Is it alcohol-free?

Yes. The bar serves kava, tea, coffee, sodas and snacks. No alcohol.

Do I have to drink kava?

No. Order anything on the menu. One item is all the venue asks.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Most people do the first time. Say it's your first night and someone will find you a game.

Do I have to play?

No. Pull up a chair and watch, or come for the conversation.
