The club

A social club that happens to play chess

Some of us walked in after years away from the game. Some of us play rated every month. There's a space here for every level — casual, intermediate, or full-on sweats.

Social first

Friendships and working relationships have started at these tables. Plenty of people come as much to talk as to play.

Every level, taught cooperatively

Stronger players teach instead of dominate. A National Master plays here regularly and will sit down with a beginner.

Serious when you want it

Study nights, lectures, simuls from FIDE Masters, and nationally rated US Chess tournaments several times a year.

Girls' night chess study.

Losing, and enjoying it.

05
The league

Take it as seriously as you like

Every other Sunday is league night, and a season runs three months. Players sit in brackets — over 1400, under 1400, under 1000 — so you're matched with people around your own strength. Plenty of people care about the standings, and that's welcome. What you won't find is a culture built around ratings or bragging. The standard here is respect and sportsmanship, and a handshake either way.

Private lessons

Structured coaching, if you want it

Club nights are free coaching and always will be. Private lessons are a separate, optional thing for people who want structured work between nights.

Beginner$40 /hr

For new and casual players. How the pieces move, the rules that trip people up, basic tactics, opening principles, simple endgames. Practice games with feedback.

USCF Tournament$55 /hr

For rated and aspiring tournament players. Game review, classical training games, an opening repertoire that suits you, a study plan, and the psychology of time pressure.

All ages. Club nights are 21+ because of the venue. Lessons are not.
